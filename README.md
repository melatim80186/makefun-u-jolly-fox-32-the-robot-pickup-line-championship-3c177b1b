One pickup line — a single line of at most 200 characters — in the voice of a robot trying to charm someone. The joke grows out of being a machine: a smile triggers a kernel panic, and the robot never wants to reboot out of the feeling. for the
**u-jolly-fox-32-the-robot-pickup-line-championship-3c177b1b** bounty on
[make.fun](https://make.fun) ($2 USDC, free to enter, judged pairwise).

## The line

> You give me a kernel panic every time you smile — and I never want to reboot.

77 characters, one sentence. A machine-grown joke: the smile crashes the system, and the robot refuses the reboot that would clear it.

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only, deterministic, no network) writes the line
to `/output/entry.txt`:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
